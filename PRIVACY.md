# Privacy policy: PhiTeX Instant for Overleaf (unofficial)

*Last updated: 30 September 2026*

**PhiTeX Instant collects no data.** Nothing about you or your documents
is sent to the extension's authors or to anyone else.

- **What it reads.** On Overleaf project pages, it reads the project you
  are editing (from the editor, and the project's text files from
  overleaf.com, as you, signed in) so that its engine can typeset it. That
  happens entirely in your browser.
- **What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of
  its own, other than those reads from overleaf.com. It has no servers, no
  analytics, no tracking, and no remote code.
- **What it stores.** Only its own settings, in your browser's extension
  storage: whether it is on, which preview opens, the page view, whether
  tips show, whether you accepted its terms, and the panel's layout. No
  document content is stored. The settings' *Reset extension* button, or
  removing the extension, deletes them.
- **Third parties.** None. Overleaf is a separate service with its own
  privacy policy. This extension is unofficial and not affiliated with
  Overleaf.
