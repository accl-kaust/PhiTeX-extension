\section{Introduction}
TeX documents are programs. Compiling one again after every keystroke is
wasteful: most of the document did not change. An incremental engine keeps
the compiled program up to date, rebuilding only what an edit touched.

\section{A live preview}
With a live preview, the page follows the text as it is typed, and the
compiled PDF remains one click away.
