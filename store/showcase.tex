{\title Instant TeX}

{\it A page that repaints as you type.}

{\head Every keystroke, incrementally}

PhiTeX compiles a TeX document into a program in SSA form, and keeps that
program up to date as the document is edited. A keystroke rebuilds only the
chunk of the document it touched; everything else is reused. The page you
are looking at is painted first, and the rest follows, to the same fixed
point a fresh build would reach.

{\head Right in your browser}

The engine runs locally, as WebAssembly: your document is never sent
anywhere. Switch between Overleaf's compiled PDF and this instant preview
whenever you like.

{\head Honest about its limits}

It is experimental and handles a subset of plain TeX today. What it cannot
read yet is listed, with a click to the line.
